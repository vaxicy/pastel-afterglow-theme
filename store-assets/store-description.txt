Pastel Afterglow Theme brings the soft light of evening to Chrome. A muted lavender frame wraps the top of the window, creamy peach softens the toolbar and active tab, and deep lavender marks the new tab header and links. The new tab page opens onto pale blush, so the page you are reading stays the brightest thing on screen. Flat solid colors throughout, with deep plum text that stays crisp on every light surface.
